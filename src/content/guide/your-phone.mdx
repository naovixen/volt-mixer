## Two jobs, two QR codes

|                      | Carry your mix                               | Phone remote                              |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| What it does         | Takes a copy of your mix to another computer | Changes levels and mutes while you listen |
| Where its QR code is | **Settings › Your mix › QR code**            | **Settings › Desktop app › Phone remote** |
| What it needs        | Nothing: works with the website, anywhere    | The desktop app, open, and the same Wi-Fi |

Why the difference: for the remote, your phone has to reach the computer the whole time you use it, and only the desktop app can listen for that. Carrying a mix is just a link, so it works everywhere.

## Carry your mix

Take your latest mix from one computer to another, with no app and no account.

1. On the computer with the mix you want, open **Settings › Your mix** and choose **QR code**.
2. Scan it with your phone's camera. Volt Mixer opens on the phone and keeps the mix.
3. At the other computer, open Volt Mixer on the phone again. It shows the mix as a QR code.
4. Scan that with the computer's camera (on Windows, the Camera app reads QR codes), or choose **Send the link** and open it on the computer.

The same happens in Firefox or Safari on a computer, since they can't talk to USB devices either: Volt Mixer keeps the mix there and says to open it in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome on the computer the Volt is plugged into.

The phone keeps the mix until you choose **Forget it**, so you can carry the same mix to any computer. Opened with nothing to carry, Volt Mixer on the phone explains both jobs and where their QR codes are:

## Phone remote

The tray mixer on your phone, for changing levels from across the room. It needs the [desktop app](../desktop-app/), open and connected to the Volt, and your phone on the same Wi-Fi.

### Set it up

1. In the desktop app, open **Settings › Desktop app** and turn on **Phone remote**.
2. With your phone on the same Wi-Fi as the computer, scan its QR code with the phone's camera.
3. The mixer opens in your phone's browser. Add it to your home screen to open it again quickly.

### What you can do from the phone

- Set the **Monitor** and **Headphones** levels, and mute them: tap a row's icon.
- Set each channel's level, and mute it, for the channels [shown in the tray](../desktop-app/#the-tray-mixer).
- **Keep this mix on my phone** takes the computer's mix with you, ready to [carry](#carry-your-mix). No second QR code needed.

Everything else, like 48V, gain and headphone routing, stays on the computer, so nothing risky can be changed from the sofa.

### The status

Above the remote's QR code, the app shows how it's doing.

| It says                                         | It means                                   |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Starting…                                       | The remote is starting up.                 |
| Waiting for a phone                             | Ready. Scan the code.                      |
| 1 phone connected                               | Shown in green while phones are in.        |
| Isn't on the network Phone remote was set up on | The remote stays off away from home.       |
| Anything in amber                               | Something is wrong. The message says what. |

The remote only answers on the network you turned it on in, usually your home Wi-Fi. Take the laptop to a café and it stays off; back home, it starts again by itself. To use it on another network, choose that network's address in the **Address** list, which appears whenever the computer is away from the remote's network or on more than one.

### Unpair phones

**New code** makes a new QR code and unpairs every phone. Each one has to scan again. Use it if you've shared the code by mistake, or a phone has gone missing.

Phones paired with version 0.5.0 or earlier need to scan the code once more after the update, as the app now checks them a safer way.

### If the phone can't connect

1. Check the phone is on the **same Wi-Fi** as the computer, not mobile data.
2. Turn off any **VPN**, on the phone and on the computer.
3. On Windows, set your home network to **Private** in **Settings › Network & internet**.
4. Allow Volt Mixer through **Windows Firewall** on private networks.

<Callout kind="caution" title="Home networks only">
    The remote's connection isn't encrypted, like most home gadgets, so someone watching your network could see your
    levels. They can't change them: the QR code's pairing code never travels over the network, and every change your
    phone sends is signed with it. Turn Phone remote off on a network you don't trust.
</Callout>
